THE HUANTED HAWELI

The Interval Start Now

There were four friends: Neha, Ranbeer, Alia, and Raheem. They were planning a trip for a few days, so they had decided to go to Lahore for a visit. The picnic was confirmed. Ranbeer said, "We have to decide on one place where we will meet together before going." Neha said, "I want to interrupt; my suggestion is that you all come to my home, then we will go to the picnic." They all agreed with Neha. Then the next day, they approached Neha's home with their own food packets and other goods for their needs. However, they kept all the bags of goods in the jeep and sat in the jeep that Ranbeer drove. They all were extremely delighted and excited for their journey. They enjoyed the fresh air with vibes of music in the car. After three hours, Alia said that she thought they had to take a break. So, Ranbeer stopped the car near a little hotel (Abdul Dhaba) and at that time, since it was evening, they preferred tea with pie (Chai, Samosa). Raheem made vlogs and took pictures of it all.

The Forest of Lost Voices

So, they started walking in search of any inn or any place to stay for the night. Due to the darkness of the night, Alia went astray from the right path and she entered a forest. She saw a huge tree around her. She tried to call her friends, but her voice was reflected back to herself. She was afraid and shouted, but her peers were very far from her. She tried to relax herself and find the way to leave the forest. On the other side, her companions didn't know that Alia was missing. When Raheem started to take pictures, he noticed that one of their partners was missing. Then they were asked each other about Alia. At that time, Ranbeer said, "Relax, we have to find Alia without wasting time." Instantly, Neha felt someone's hand on her shoulder; she shouted and turned, and it was Alia's hand. Alia seemed terrified. She told them she was lost in the forest. Ranbeer said, "It is all okay, let's move to find any suitable place for a stay."

The Haveli of Harshwardhan

After covering enough distance, Raheem saw a light coming from afar. They followed the brightness and finally approached a huge, palace-like building. They opened the door. Abruptly, a crowd of bats attacked them. Neha and Alia were frightened, but Raheem put out his camera for pictures of the adventurous bats. When they entered, they saw a large painting of a king and his servant on the wall. The caption of the painting read, "Haveli of Harshwardhan." Raheem took so many pictures of this painting and also the haveli. There was too much dust and spiderwebs everywhere. At first, they decided to clean the sofas for rest. When they started dusting, out of the blue, all the windows were loudly shut and all the bulbs were damaged. They all were petrified, but Ranbeer tried to handle the situation and he went to open the shut windows. When he opened a window, a haunted red moon, half-covered with long branches of trees in the dark night, appeared in front of him. It was truly a terrifying moment. He ignored it and joined his companions.

The Loyal Soldier

They put out their dinner from their bags and ate.Suddenly, an unknown person came from the room. They were shocked and thought about how he could have come from the room when this haveli was closed. Ranbeer asked the man, "Who are you and how are you here?" The person replied, "This is the house of my owner and I live here." Raheem took a picture with him and then asked, "For how much time have you lived there, and who is your owner?" He replied, "I have lived there since 1000 years," and he pointed to the painting of the king and said, "He is my owner and I am his loyal soldier, always ready to protect him and his property. So, never try to steal anything from his palace." Raheem laughed and said, "A nice story, isn't it?" Raheem went to his friend and said that he thought the person was mentally unhealthy. Ranbeer said, "Stop this nonsense." When Ranbeer and his friend turned, there was nothing. They thought that maybe he had gone. They didn't notice the servant had disappeared from the painting.

A Night of Terror

It was late at night, 2 AM, so they wanted to take a rest. They were asleep, and after an hour, Raheem felt that someone pushed his shoulder. The first time he ignored it and slept again, but it repeated again and again, so he woke up and tried to awake his friends, but no one took him seriously. Raheem saw the previous stranger, who went towards the stairs. He silently followed him. Instantly, he disappeared, and Raheem was finding him. He felt someone standing over him. He was scared. When he stood at the topmost stair, suddenly someone pushed him and he fell down from the stairs. Due to this sound, Ranbeer woke up and he helped Raheem to stand. Raheem shared his experience with Ranbeer, but he didn't take it seriously, and they went back to sleep. Raheem was scared, so he was sleeping with the light on. Then Ranbeer felt something strange. He also saw the stranger. When he called him, the stranger didn't turn. He only twisted his neck at a 360-degree angle. Ranbeer shouted and ran away from him. He went to their companions and told them, "Wake up and leave this palace because it is a haunted haveli. We must never spend one minute in this haveli."

The Revelation of Light

All were afraid, but they left the palace and sat together outside the haveli. At that time at 4:30 AM, they decided to wait for morning and then leave the forest. Since they were waiting for morning, they watched the pictures which were taken by Raheem. They saw the pictures one by one, but Neha noticed a picture which was taken with the stranger. When they all noticed this picture, there was nothing beside Raheem; instead, they saw a big black shadow over Raheem. And also, they noticed the first and last pictures of the painting. They pondered that in the first picture, the servant was present in the painting, and in the last picture, no one was in the painting except the king. Then, at that time, they understood the whole situation of fear. Then they saw the sun shine. The dark night of fear was over, and the journey of light started from that new morning.

As the golden sun climbed the horizon, the shadows of the past dissolved, leaving them with the silent, chilling truth that some stories never truly end—they only wait in the darkness for the next traveler.
